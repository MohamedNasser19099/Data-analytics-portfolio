# Car Sales Performance Dashboard

## Overview
This project analyzes sales performance of car to evaluate year to date results, highlight growth drivers and highlight areas for improving revenue and operational efficiency using an PowerBI dashboard.

## Business objective
Understand the car sales performance of this year and highlight key growth drivers and identify where to put the effort to enhance revenue and efficiency.

## Data Source
The dataset contains transactions of car sales, including date of sales, vehicle body style, color, company/brand, location, quantity sold and revenue.
The data was analyzed and visualized using Power BI.

## Tools Used
-PowerBI

## Key KPIs
- Total YTD Sales: $371.2M
- Year-over-Year Sales Growh: 23.6%
- Cars Sold YTD: 13.3k
- Year-over-Year volume Growth: 24.6%
- Average Selling Price: -79% YOY

- ## What Happened (Performance Summary)
- Strong overall sales growth generated from higher sales volume
- Revenue growth is not generated from price increases
- Average selling price decreased YOY

## key Drivers (Why It Happened)
### product Performance
- SUVS are the top body style in performance
- Hard top models have the lowest sales performance
 
### Color Preference
- Pale White is the best color in sales
- Red is the lowest color in sales

### Geographic Performance
- Austine is the best place in the number of cars sold
- Middletown is the worst place in the number of cars sold

### Time-Based Trends
- Week 36 is the strongest sales week
- Week 2 is the weakest Sales week

### Company / Brand Performance
- Chevrolet is the top brand in sales
- Jaguar is the lowest brand in sales

## Business Risks Identified
- Sales growth is from the number of car sold While average prices are decreased
- Potential pressure on the profits
- places with low performance can increase inventory and cost risks

## Recommendations (What Should be done)
### Product Strategy
-Increase production OF SUV models
- Review marketing, pricing and keep producing of hardtop models

### Inventory Strategy
- Increase stock of Pale White vehicles
- Decreace the production of red vehicles

### Regional Strategy
- Invest in regions with high performance as Austin
- Investigate low performance regions as Middletown

### Timing & Promotions
- Run promotions during low sales times (Week 2)
- increase inventory in high sales times (week 36)

### Brand Strategy
- focus on partnerships with top performance brands such as chevrolet
- Make new strategy for price and market for jaguar

## Business Value Added
- Highlights key drivers of profitability
- Highlights for margin risks caused by declining average prices
- Supports data driven decisions for inventory, pricing and regional strategy
- support strategic planning and operational efficiency
